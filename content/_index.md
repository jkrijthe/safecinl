## Who we are

We are researchers interested in causal inference, and in making sure that the claims we make are substantiated with evidence, so that we can make better-informed decisions.

Safe Causal Inference is an [NWO project](https://www.nwo.nl/en/projects/ddpuz06393) running from 2025 to 2031. Many of us also take part in the [Causal Inference for AI](https://www.causalinference.nl/) meetings, an informal research community in the Netherlands.

{{< partners >}}

## What we do

We develop tools and methodologies that help other researchers and practitioners come to causal conclusions safely.
